# Fictional Hiring Policy: Inclusive Language

Use clear, respectful language focused on the work and its requirements. Describe skills, responsibilities, and experience that are relevant to the role.

Do not include eligibility requirements or preferences based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, marital status, family status, or other personal characteristics unrelated to the job.

Avoid gender-coded wording, stereotypes, and language that suggests only one type of person belongs in the role. Use gender-neutral terms such as “they” and “you.”

Include an equal-opportunity statement. Do not ask applicants to disclose sensitive personal information in a job description.
